On-Campus Highlights

When it comes to campus life, Syracuse definitely knows how to keep students entertained! From Juice Jam and Block Party to Welcome Week on the Quad and Quad-a-Palooza, there is always something to look forward to throughout the school year. If you are looking for something beyond the usual parties, Geek Con, the Lunar New Year Celebration, Carib Fest, and the Outlaws Showcase are also events worth keeping on your radar. Of course, the experience would not be complete without getting involved with campus organizations. The African Student Union (ASU) welcomes all students and hosts events throughout the year, while the Caribbean Student Association (CSA) brings the culture with its annual Carib Fest showcase every spring. For those focused on professional development, NABA and NSBE offer annual conference opportunities and year-round networking events to help students build connections and explore career opportunities. And for students looking to connect through shared religious or spiritual interests, campus communities offer another way to find your people.

Off-Campus Highlights

Now, if you thought all the excitement stayed on campus, think again! Syracuse’s off-campus social scene has events of its own. HHH events are known for taking place at larger venues featuring performers and some of the best music around. Then there are the Prett Iced OWT dirties/parties, hosted by three of the Divine Nine fraternities once a semester. Both are off-campus events, so do not confuse them with university-sponsored activities. Other events to keep on your radar include the All Gold Darty and White Boy Wasted.
